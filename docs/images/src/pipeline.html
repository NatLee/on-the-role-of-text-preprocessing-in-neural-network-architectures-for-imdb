$preproc-imdb train -p vanilla -m cnn-lstm
1 · IMDb reviews
50,000 reviews
25,000 to train, 25,000 to test, half positive and half negative (Maas et al., 2011)
one review per line in
data/IMDb/<variant>/
2 · Preprocessing variant
vanillaApple is asking … United States . lowercasedapple is asking … united states . lemmatizedApple be ask … United States . multiwordApple is asking … United_States .
Text preprocessed by the paper's authors (Stanford CoreNLP, Google News phrases). multiword ships with the repo, download adds the rest.
3 · Word ids
[0 0 … 2 61 1 845]
Training words get ids 2, 3, … by frequency; 0 pads, 1 marks an unknown word. 20,000 ids in all.
Padded or cut at the start, to the longest review by default.
4 · Embedding
20,000 × 300
Random, or the authors' UMBC word2vec vectors with the same preprocessing.
5 · Model
cnncnn-lstm
Convolution and max pooling, with or without a BiLSTM, then one sigmoid unit.
6 · Evaluation
Test accuracy
All 25,000 test reviews, mean over --runs seeds, saved to results.json.
